Privacy Policy for Rathod Cars Mitra

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

MithTechX ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Rathod Cars Mitra mobile application and website (rathodcarsmitra.com). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our intercity ride-booking service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Phone numbers, names, and account credentials required for user authentication and ride booking.
  • Location Data: Pickup and drop-off locations, routes, and geographic coordinates necessary to calculate fares and match intercity cab services.
  • Transaction Data: Payment confirmation references and booking history securely handled via Supabase and integrated payment gateways.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and manage your ride bookings.
  • To notify you about status updates regarding your trips.
  • To provide customer support and resolve booking issues.
  • To monitor usage and prevent fraudulent activities.

3. Data Security & Storage

All application data, including user profiles and bookings, are stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure with encrypted database connections and Row Level Security (RLS).

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses trusted third-party services (such as Supabase) that adhere to strict data protection standards. We do not sell or rent your personal identification information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 18.
