Privacy Policy & Service Terms for Rathod Cars Mitra

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

MithTechX ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Rathod Cars Mitra mobile application and website (rathodcarsmitra.com). This page outlines our privacy practices and core booking and cancellation policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Phone numbers, names, and account credentials required for user authentication and ride booking.
  • Location Data: Pickup and drop-off locations, routes, and geographic coordinates necessary to calculate fares and match intercity cab services.
  • Transaction Data: Payment confirmation references and booking history securely handled via Supabase and integrated payment gateways.

2. Ride Booking & Confirmation Policy

  • Booking Submission: Placing a ride request or booking does not mean the ride is automatically confirmed.
  • Operator Acceptance: Rides are officially reviewed, accepted, and confirmed by the service provider before payment continuation or final dispatch.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Operator Cancellation: If a customer has completed a payment and the operator cancels the ride, a 100% full refund will be issued.
  • Pre-Confirmation Cancellation: If a customer requests a cancellation after booking but before official confirmation, it is permitted subject to a 10% cancellation charge.
  • Post-Confirmation Cancellation: Once a ride has been officially booked and confirmed by the operator, customer cancellations are not permitted and non-refundable.

4. Data Security & Storage

All application data, including user profiles and bookings, are stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure with encrypted database connections and Row Level Security (RLS).

5. Third-Party Services

Our app uses trusted third-party services (such as Supabase and payment gateways) that adhere to strict data protection standards. We do not sell or rent your personal identification information.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 18.
